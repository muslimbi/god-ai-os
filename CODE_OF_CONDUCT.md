# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in GOD AI OS respectful, constructive, inclusive, and technically focused.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Discuss ideas, not people.
- Provide evidence for technical claims.
- Welcome good-faith disagreement.
- Avoid harassment or discrimination.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Threats
- Deliberate disruption
- Malicious behavior toward project infrastructure

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate discussions, remove harmful content, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
